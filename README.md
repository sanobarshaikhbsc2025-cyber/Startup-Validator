# ⚔️ Startup Battle-Bot

> AI-powered startup validation platform that roasts, validates, and transforms business ideas into investor-ready frameworks.

Startup Battle-Bot analyzes a raw startup idea and generates structured insights across validation, competition, branding, and investor pitching.

Built using Large Language Models, Prompt Engineering, Python, and Streamlit.

---

## 🚀 Features

### ⚔️ 10-Point Battle Diagnostic

Evaluate a startup idea across 10 critical dimensions including:

- Problem Clarity
- Revenue Model
- Market Size
- Competitive Moat
- and other critical startup criteria

Each dimension receives a score from 1–10 along with strengths, risks, and an overall verdict.

### 🔍 Competitor Research

Analyze the competitive landscape and identify:

- Direct competitors
- Indirect competitors
- Potential competitors
- Competitor strengths and weaknesses
- Market gaps
- Blue-ocean opportunities

### 🎨 Brand Identity Generator

Generate a complete startup brand identity including:

- Brand name
- Tagline
- Color palette
- Logo concept
- Brand story
- Digital handles
- Target persona

### 📊 Investor Pitch Deck

Generate a professional 5-slide investor pitch structure containing:

- Elevator pitch
- Market information
- Business model
- Investor hook
- Speaker notes
